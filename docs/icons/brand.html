Words and Wounds — brand & launcher icon

What Google's “G” is called, and everything we have for the app: the symbol, the name, the home-screen icon and its colour versions.

Logomark

The symbol on its own — Google's “G”. Ours: the stitched open book.

Wordmark

The name written out in a fixed style — Google's lettered “Google”. Ours: “Words and Wounds”.

Lockup

Logomark + wordmark arranged together (side by side, or stacked).

App icon (launcher icon)

The logomark placed on a coloured square that the phone masks to a circle or rounded shape. This is what you tap on the home screen.
